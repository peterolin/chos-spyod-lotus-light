# Request to Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling — permission and blessing for the chos spyod eBook

Draft, 2026-09-17. One page in each language. Names in brackets are for Peter to
fill in; the Tibetan uses honorific register throughout and is addressed to a

## English

**To [Khenpo … / the Publications Office of Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling]**

Dear Khenpo-la,

For some years I have been preparing an eBook edition of the monastery's
chant book, ཉེར་མཁོའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་གཅེས་བཏུས་ཐར་པའི་ཐེམ་སྐས།, so that practitioners at
Ka-Nying and at the Gomde centres can recite from a phone or tablet. I am
writing to ask for the monastery's permission, and for your blessing.

**What the edition is.** The Tibetan text follows the third printing, page for
page, with the printed page numbers kept beside every title. Nothing in the
text has been changed or omitted. What the editor has added is set apart in
red and explained on a page of its own: links that lead from one prayer to the
next in the order they are recited, marks for repetitions, and the lines the
print abbreviates written out. A supplement at the end holds a few prayers
recited at Ka-Nying that are not in the printed book, taken from the Lumbini
2024 chant book. The eBook is free and will remain free; it is not sold.

**What I am asking.**

1. Permission to distribute the edition without charge, from its download
   page and, if the monastery agrees, through the free sections of the online
   bookstores, listed under the monastery's name with myself as editor.
2. Your blessing for the edition, and the guidance of a lama on a small
   number of questions about the order of recitation where the book offers a
   link — at present six places in the Zabtik Drolchok, which are marked in
   the book itself.

**What I undertake in return.** The title page will name Ka-Nying Shedrub
Ling and state that the edition is authorised. Anything the monastery asks me
to change or remove, I will change or remove. Every future version will be
sent for review before it is released, and the monastery may withdraw its
permission at any time.

The current version is attached so that it can be read on a phone. I would be
glad to come and show it, and to answer any question.

With respect and gratitude,

Peter Olin
Editor of the eBook; translator, Khyentse Vision Project
[place, date] · chosspyod.wordpress.com · [email]

### Notes on the Tibetan

- Register: honorific throughout (ཞུ་, སྩལ་, གནང་, ཞལ་བཀོད་, སྐུ་མདུན་). The writer
  refers to himself as ཁོ་བོ, the plain first person of a letter to a superior.
- "Permission" is rendered བཀའ་འཁྲོལ, the standing word for an authorisation
  granted from above; "blessing" བྱིན་རླབས; "guidance of a lama" ཞལ་བཀོད.
- "eBook" is glossed གློག་རྡུལ་དེབ with the English in brackets on first use,
  since usage varies; the monastery may have its own word.
- "Third printing" is པར་གཞི་གསུམ་པ; "authorised edition" བཀའ་འཁྲོལ་ཐོབ་པའི་དཔར་མ.
- The two numbered requests use the Tibetan enumeration ༡༏ ༢༏ so the reader
  sees at once that there are exactly two.
- Peter to check the rendering of the KVP name (མཁྱེན་བརྩེའི་གཟིགས་སྣང་ལས་འཆར་) against
  the project's own Tibetan name before sending.
