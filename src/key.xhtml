Notes From the Editor

This is an attempt to faithfully represent the pecha version of the chos spyod as an eBook, useful on small devices as well as on larger screens.

This prayer book is used in a variety of contexts, and the main aim is to provide a convenient, alternative source for those who don't have the printed version at hand. Whether used personally, or in group practices, an e-book reader does not provide the convenience of a physical book when it comes to the possibility of adding inserted notes and bookmarks, quickly flicking through pages to often used prayers, having multiple pages visible at once, and so forth. So to make the eBook useful, elements for ease of reading and navigation have been added. The aim is to make it completely clear what those changes and additions are, so that there can be no doubt about what is in the printed chos spyod, and what has been added in this e-book version for the convenience of the reader. Below is a table describing the addition made, and the typography that sets it apart from the original.
