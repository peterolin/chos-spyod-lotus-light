པར་བྱང་། Colophon

This eBook is an independent, unofficial edition of the chos spyod of Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling, ཉེར་མཁོའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་གཅེས་བཏུས་ཐར་པའི་ཐེམ་སྐས། It follows the third printing. Later printings differ from it in small readings, and the texts they added, the Ultimate Guru Sadhana of Simplicity and Calling the Guru from Afar, are included here in the ཟུར་ཡིག at the end, with a few other prayers not in the printed book.

Its text descends from an EPUB found on the Internet, auto-generated from what was probably a word-processor file of the printed book, and not from the publisher's source. That file was cleaned, restructured and given its navigation here. Everything the editor added is marked as such; see How to read this book.

Sources of added text. The prayers in the ཟུར་ཡིག are taken from the Lumbini 2024 Ka-Nying chantbook. The line of symbolic script that opens the རྒྱུན་གྱི་བཀོལ་བྱང་། is reproduced from the Rinchen Terdzö. The Tibetan is set in Monlam Uni OuChan2 from the Monlam IT Research Centre, free to use.
