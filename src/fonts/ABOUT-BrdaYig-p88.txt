BrdaYig-p88.ttf

A one-glyph font. The köljang (chos spyod p. 88) opens with a short line of
ḍākinī script (བརྡ་ཡིག), a few stacked signs that are not Tibetan letters and
that Unicode cannot spell. The signs are traced from the Rinchen Terdzö,
where the same line stands clean, and mapped to U+E000 (Private Use Area).
Set as text they take the reader's ink in every theme, which no image can
do in Apple Books' Night theme (it repaints text, and leaves images alone).

Scaled so that the gter tsheg ༔ closing the line matches Monlam's (0.607 em,
top on the head line at 472/1000); the signs then run from 0.49 to -0.46 of
the em, as tall as the letters with their descenders. Made
2026-09-16 with fontTools from a pixel-edge trace of the bitmap; free to use
with the book.
